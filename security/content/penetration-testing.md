This section describes the CadenzaFlow's penetration testing policy, and provides penetration testing results.


# Penetration Testing

CadenzaFlow plans to engage an independent external security advisor to perform regular penetration testing and security assessments of the platform.
This section will be updated as the assessment methodology, scope, frequency, engagement model, and related processes are finalized.
Once available, the results and summaries of conducted penetration tests will also be published in the following section.
